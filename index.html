Every travel decision, optimized — not just the fare.

Weighs cost, comfort, upgrade potential, award value, flexibility, and risk together, based on what matters most on this trip. Give it the trip and what's in your wallet — miles, points, cards — and it tells you what to book, and why.

Point valuations and transfer bonuses are real, dated figures from The Points Guy (July 2026), refreshed by hand. See the guide for exactly what's computed vs. copied vs. planned.
