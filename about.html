01

what it is

You give it a trip — origin, destination, dates, cabin, budget, loyalty balances, and a ranked list of what matters most to you (cheapest, most comfortable, best upgrade shot, best use of miles, and so on). It runs every candidate itinerary through the same six-dimension scoring framework — Cost, Comfort, Upgrade Potential, Award Value, Flexibility, Risk — weighted by your ranking, and explains exactly why the winner won, not just what it costs.

It never optimizes for a single variable. A itinerary that's $40 cheaper but meaningfully worse on the dimensions you ranked higher won't out-score the better-rounded option, and the page shows you the weight breakdown so that trade-off is never hidden.

02

the stack

Static HTML with a deterministic client-side scoring engine — no backend, no build step, nothing leaves your browser. That's a deliberate v1 choice, not a limitation discovered later: the scoring framework, weighting logic, and every output section (Executive Summary, Best Recommendation, Top Five Alternatives, Upgrade/Award/Purchase Strategy, Advisor Insights) are all real and computed from your inputs today. What's not real yet is the fare and award data underneath them — see the stack comparison below.

This site, today

  • Static HTML, deterministic scoring engine, no build step
  • Reference ranges for fares/miles, hand-maintained, not fetched
  • No Netlify Functions yet — nothing to fetch live
  • Every run is reproducible: same inputs, same result

Vinyl Scout & The Fitness Log

  • Full Netlify Functions backend
  • Live data sources (WHOOP API, Discogs pricing)
  • Automated backups & health checks
  • GitHub repo, auto-deploy on push

Live flight and award search (Google Flights/ITA-style fare data, seats.aero-style award availability) is Phase 2 — see the roadmap. Until then, every price and mileage figure on this page is labeled as a planning range from a documented model, the same honesty standard the other two sites hold themselves to when a data source isn't real yet.

03

what's on the page

Input Card

Trip details, flight preferences, ranked priorities, credit cards, loyalty balances, and airport flexibility — everything the engine weighs.

Executive Summary & Best Recommendation

One paragraph on exactly what to book and why, plus the winning itinerary's full detail card.

Decision Framework

The six scored dimensions with the exact weight your priority ranking assigned to each — the weighting is always shown, never hidden.

Strategy blocks

Upgrade, Award, and Purchase Timing strategy, plus 5–8 advisor-style insights specific to your inputs.

04

roadmap

Built in phases, like the other two sites. The scoring framework and full page set are live; live fare/award data, a backend, and a real domain are next. See the full roadmap and the guide for exactly what's computed vs. planned.

05

what it won't do

  • Won't present a reference-range price or mileage figure as a live quote — every number here is labeled for what it is.
  • Won't recommend redeeming miles unless the computed value clears a genuinely strong bar (~1.6¢/mile) — see the Award Strategy section's own recommendation logic.
  • Won't optimize for a single variable — price alone never wins if it costs too much on the dimensions you ranked higher.
  • No accounts, no passwords, no analytics or third-party tracking, nothing sent off your device.
